Wiring: MIDI & CV

What talks to what

Install

  1. Copy sci.lua to dust/code/sci/sci.lua (maiden, SMB or USB stick).
  2. On norns: SELECT → sci. The title shows for 2.5 s, then the guide view.
  3. First run moves any old dust/data/mpecap saves to dust/data/sci.

The norns screen

  • Guide view (default): page tabs, play status, 4 lines on rows 1–6 of this page, and an alternating row 7/8 line.
  • Press any grid key: the bottom line names it for 3 s, e.g. r7 k3 BRAKE (hold).
  • Tap K1 alone to switch to the reels view and back.

norns settings

  • SYSTEM → DEVICES → MIDI: assign LinnStrument, Electra Controller 1 and 2, U2MIDI, nanoKEY2, nanoPAD2 and the shield's TRS MIDI port.
  • SYSTEM → AUDIO: monitor all the way down.

Electra One Mini

app.electra.one → Controller → Configuration:

  • Router: USB Device Port 1 → USB Host Port 1 (Nymphes) and USB Device Port 2 → USB Host Port 2 (Micromonsta 2).
  • USB Host: Nymphes → Port 1, Micromonsta → Port 2, on a powered hub. (The Mini takes 2 host devices: now full.)

sci tracks (defaults)

1NymphesElectra Controller 1 · ch 2–7
2Micromonsta 2Electra Controller 2 · ch 2–7: set to its MPE voices
3HadesU2MIDI · ch 2 only (mono)
4spareElectra Controller 1

Thru follows the selected track; on a one-channel track (the Hades) your notes move to its channel, without bend. The nanoKEY plays track 3 and the nanoPAD2 pads 36/38 play the Alecto lanes (PARAMS → controllers); both go on the USB hub.

Synths

  • LinnStrument: normal MPE layout, member channels 2–7, bend range to match the Nymphes.
  • Nymphes: MPE on (firmware 2.0+). Match sci's synth bend range to it.
  • Micromonsta 2: MPE, firmware V3 (works on the Mini's USB host). Set track 2's member channels to its voice count.
  • Hades: MIDI in (5-pin DIN) from the U2MIDI. It listens on all channels until you set its internal DIP switch: switch 3 on = channel 2. Mono, notes C-1 to C4.
  • ZOIA Euroburo: the patch on the ZOIA page (from the ZOIA build guide). sci's drum out is the shield's TRS port; the ZOIA is TRS Type A.

Check it works

  1. Track 1 + thru: the Nymphes plays, slides in tune. Track 2: the Micromonsta. Track 3: the Hades.
  2. DRUM page, add a step, PLAY: the Alecto fires.
  3. AUDIO page, REC a few bars: it plays back.

Everything into the L6max, with the ZOIA inline

The ZOIA sits inline, as in the ZOIA build guide: each synth has its own crusher, and the mix knob on each chain takes it fully clean. Use the −20 dB pad on 5–8 for hot sources.

AUX 1: what sci records

  • AUX SEND 1 → norns input, mono TS cable into the left input.
  • Press AUX1, raise the synths you want to loop or print (Nymphes, Micromonsta, Hades…).
  • Keep ch 8 (norns return) at 0, or it feeds back.
  • Ch 1 and 3 arrive already crushed, so sci records the crushed sound; set the ZOIA mix to clean to record them dry.

AUX 2

  • Free. The ZOIA no longer needs a send; it is patched inline between the Hades/Alecto and ch 1/3.

Levels

  • The Hades out is hot (10 Vpp): start the ZOIA's audio in L pad at −12 dB, move to −6 or 0 if it's quiet.
  • Hades cable: 6.3 mm to 3.5 mm mono. ZOIA outs: 3.5 mm to 6.3 mm mono into ch 1 and 3.
  • norns SYSTEM → AUDIO: monitor all the way down.

Pedals

Your Komorebi and Darkness can stay on the main out as before, so they process the whole mix.

ZOIA patch recipe

The same patch as the ZOIA build guide (Layout A: dynamic kit), in short

Page 1 · MIDI to Alecto

  1. Midi Notes In: channel 10, 1 output, priority newest, velocity output on, trigger pulse on, low and high note C1 (36).
  2. Its trigger out → CV out 1 → Alecto TRIG 1. Its velocity out → CV out 3 → Alecto VELO 1.
  3. A second Midi Notes In on the next row, the same but low and high note D1 (38): trigger → CV out 2 → TRIG 2, velocity → CV out 4 → VELO 2.
  4. Each CV out: Edit → out range 0 to 5 V, in range 0 to 1. Save.

Plugging into a TRIG jack stops that voice's own sequencer: sci is the sequencer now. VELO is at maximum with nothing plugged in, so velocity can only turn a voice down. If a pad doesn't fire, try the notes an octave up or down; if hits are missed, use gate out instead of trigger out.

Page 2 · crushers

  1. Top row: Bit Crusher → Aliaser (the ZOIA's decimator) → Audio Balance (mono) in 2.
  2. Audio in L → Bit Crusher in, and → Audio Balance in 1 (dry). Audio Balance out → Audio out L.
  3. Second row: the same chain on audio in R → audio out R for the drums.
  4. Audio in L pad −12 dB (the Hades is hot). Mix: bass about halfway, drums further toward crushed.

  • Add Midi CC In modules on channel 10 for the X-Y pad's CCs (often CC 1 and 2; check the Korg Kontrol Editor) and connect them to crushed bits, alias amount or mix.

Quick start

Your first loops, in order

1 · A MIDI loop

  1. Row 8 key 5: select track 1. Row 8 key 12: loop length 4 bars.
  2. Press REC (row 8 key 1, or K2). Playback starts on the next beat and REC blinks.
  3. Play the LinnStrument. Each note joins the loop when you lift your finger, with its bend, slide and pressure.
  4. Keep playing to overdub. UNDO (row 8 key 3) removes the last pass.
  5. Press REC again to stop recording. The loop keeps playing.

2 · Edit it

  • On the MIDI page, notes appear on rows 1–6. K1+E3 scrolls pitch.
  • Tap a note to delete it, tap an empty key to add one, hold a note and tap further right to change its length.
  • Hold a key and play the LinnStrument to step-record a note there.

3 · Mangle the tape

  • Row 7 key 3 BRAKE (hold): the loop slows to a stop with a pitch dive. Release to spin back up.
  • Row 7 key 1 REVERSE: every note plays backwards, gesture and all.
  • Row 7 keys 9–16: speeds from ¼× to 2×. Key 5 RESYNC snaps back to 1× on the beat.

4 · An audio loop

  1. Row 7 key 8 cycles pages. Tabs across the top of the norns screen show which page the grid is on, and the page name flashes large for a moment when you switch. Go to LOOP.
  2. Track 2's big button (cols 6–7, rows 3–4): press = REC, again = DUB, again = PLAY.
  3. Or use PRINT to record track 1's MIDI, as played by the Nymphes, into track 1's audio for one lap. Then change the patch and play something new on top.

5 · A beat

  1. Go to the DRUM page. Tap steps on row 1 (Alecto voice 1) and row 4 (voice 2).
  2. Hold a step about ½ s for an accent.
  3. Give lane 2 a different length (row 5) or clock (row 6) for polymeter.
  4. Or press REC (row 8 key 1) and play the nanoPAD2: each hit lands on the nearest step, hard hits become accents.

6 · Perform

  • QUANT page row 1: launch quantize to bar, so starts, stops and mutes land on the next bar.
  • QUANT row 6: tape drives audio only. Your MIDI keeps playing the Nymphes in time while you brake and reverse the audio.

7 · Save

  • SAVE page: pick a slot on row 1, hold SAVE (row 2 key 1) for 1 s.

Steps (rows 1 and 4)

TapStep on/off.
Hold ~½ sAccent on/off (also turns the step on). Bright = accent, medium = normal, dark = off.
Moving barEach lane's current step lights its whole column at the lowest brightness, across that lane's 3 rows: a bar of light sweeping left to right. Lanes with different lengths or clocks sweep at their own pace. Steps that are on flash full as the bar passes.

Length & clock (rows 2–3, 5–6)

LengthTap 1–16. Steps past the length go dark.
Clock1/32 to 1 bar per step, including triplets. Different lengths or clocks per lane = polymeter, like the Alecto's own sequencer.
ShiftRotate the lane one step left/right within its length.
MUTELit = playing. Uses launch quantize (blinks while waiting).

Main row on this page

PLAYDrums start with the transport, step 1 on the downbeat, and stop with it.
UNDOSteps back through drum edits: steps, accents, length, clock, shift, clear.
CLEARHold 1 s: clears both lanes' steps. Undo brings them back.
RECRecords the nanoPAD2 into the lanes: each hit goes to the nearest step of its lane, and each step replays at the velocity you hit it (hits at or above accent from velocity, 100, light as accents). X-Y pad moves are recorded too. A hit just before a step plays once, not twice. UNDO removes the whole pass.

Timing

Drums run on the clock, not the tape. Brake and varispeed bend the loops, but the beat holds.

Good to know

Filesdust/data/sci/slotNN/: session.data (MIDI + settings), t1–t4.wav (audio), params.pset. The wavs open in any audio editor.

Troubleshooting & limits

What to check

No sound from the Nymphes

  • PARAMS: synth out = Electra Controller 1, thru = on.
  • Electra router: USB Device Port 1 → USB Host Port 1, and the Nymphes is on host Port 1.
  • Track's MIDI on (AUDIO/LOOP/QUANT pages) isn't muted.

Micromonsta or Hades silent

  • Micromonsta: router rule USB Device Port 2 → USB Host Port 2, and it's on host Port 2.
  • Hades: track 3's synth out is the U2MIDI, DIP switch 3 on (ch 2), notes within C-1 to C4. If it plays drum notes, it's still on OMNI.

nanoKEY or nanoPAD2 not responding

  • PARAMS → controllers: check nanoKEY in and nanoPAD2 in. They can't use the LinnStrument's port.
  • nanoPAD2: the pads must send notes 36 and 38 (or change the pad notes in PARAMS).

Slides out of tune

  • The two bend-range PARAMS must match the LinnStrument and the synth.

Alecto silent

  • drum out = the shield's TRS MIDI port; the Euroburo is listening on ch 10, notes 36/38 (try an octave up or down). TRS Type A vs B: see the ZOIA build guide.
  • Very quiet: VELO can only turn down. Unplug the VELO cables to compare.
  • Lane not muted (row 3/6 key 16 lit); transport playing.
  • Raise trigger length.

Audio loops silent or very quiet

  • Send level up on the Nymphes channel (AUX1), cable into the norns input.
  • Balanced TRS into a stereo input can cancel: use a mono TS cable into the left input.
  • Track not stopped (LOOP page) and fader up.

Hearing the Nymphes twice

  • norns SYSTEM → AUDIO → monitor down.

Feedback

  • The norns return channel's AUX1 send must be 0.

Known limits

  • Audio is mono, up to 55 s per loop (8 bars at about 35 bpm).
  • One loop length for all tracks. Changing bars or tempo after recording audio misaligns it; SAVE/LOAD restores both.
  • Audio undo is one step (press again to redo).
  • The Hades is mono and plays C-1 to C4; live thru drops pitch bend on it.
  • The Mini's USB host is full with the Nymphes and Micromonsta 2.
  • Drums run on the clock and don't follow the tape.
  • Single-track loads only go into the same track number.
  • Tested in a simulated norns; first hardware run may need tweaks.

How the code works

A section-by-section tour of sci.lua, in file order

1 · Header, constants and tracks

The comment block at the top is the control reference for every page. Below it are the constants (tick size, speeds, pages, drum clock divisions) and the tracks table with four entries. Each track holds its MIDI notes (pattern), undo history, output port, and audio state (has_audio, gain, rev, fx…). One track = one MIDI loop + one audio loop.

2 · State and helpers

The working variables: notes being recorded (live), fingers down (held), tape position (head) and speed, pending quantized actions, the drum lanes, the selected save slot. snap rounds positions so floating-point drift can't break note timing; follow_pitch scrolls the editor.

3 · MIDI recording

on_midi handles everything from the LinnStrument. A note-on starts a live note; bend, slide and pressure add timestamped points to its curve. commit finalizes the note when you lift your finger, flipping the curve if the tape ran backwards. With thru on, what you play is forwarded to the synth.

4 · MIDI playback

trigger_notes checks every tick which notes start between the previous and current tape position (in_span handles the loop wrap) and calls start_voice. alloc hands out member channels round-robin, skipping channels your fingers are using. advance_voices steps each playing note through its curve. bend_value converts bend ranges and adds the tape pitch drop. q_start applies note quantize at playback time.

5 · Audio (softcut)

Each track has its own softcut voice and buffer regions (loop, backup, temp). The a_ functions handle record, overdub, undo-as-swap (so a second undo is redo), clear and the 505 state cycle (a_state, a_big). a_rate sets each voice's speed from the tape. on_wrap runs when the loop restarts, which is how PRINT records exactly one lap.

6 · Tape motion

ticker runs tape_tick 96 times per beat. Each tick it runs due quantized actions, eases speed toward the target (a linear ramp for brake and spin-up), adds flutter, moves head, triggers notes and steps the drums. Tape drives decides whether MIDI, audio or both follow the motion.

7 · Transport and actions

Start, stop, record, undo, clear, track select and loop length. quantized is the launch-quantize wrapper: it queues an action for the next beat or bar and cancels it if the same key is pressed again.

8 · Note editing and grid pages

The MIDI editor (add, delete, length), then each page's draw and key functions: AUDIO, LOOP, DRUM (drum_step runs on its own tick counter, so drums ignore the tape; it also draws the moving bar), SAVE (session.data for MIDI and settings, a .wav per track) and QUANT. grid_redraw and g.key come last because they call every page.

9 · norns UI and startup

key and enc handle buttons and encoders; redraw draws the reels, status line and the 2.5 s title; init builds PARAMS, migrates old saves, starts softcut and the tick loop.
